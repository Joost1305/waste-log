How to use the app

For kitchen staff and students. Registering one item of waste takes about 20 seconds. This page shows you how.

1. Getting started

  1. Accept your invitationYou receive an e-mail from WASTE log. Tap the link and choose your own password.
  2. Sign inOpen the app link on your phone and sign in with your e-mail address and password.
  3. Put the app on your home screeniPhone (Safari): tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android (Chrome): tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen. You now open WASTE log with one tap, like a normal app.
  4. Choose your languageTap EN or NL at the top. The app remembers your choice.
Forgot your password? On the sign-in page, fill in your e-mail address and tap Forgot your password?. You get an e-mail with a link to choose a new one. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes.

2. Register waste, step by step

The app opens on Register. The step you still need to fill in has a green outline. Finished steps get a check mark. Just follow the green.

  1. Restaurant and sectionIf you work in more than one restaurant, tap the right one at the top. If your restaurant has sections (for example Grill, Salad bar, Pizza & pasta), tap your section. Your phone remembers both for next time.
  2. PhotoTap Take photo to use the camera, or Choose from photos to pick a photo you already took. A photo is not required, but it helps a lot (see below).
  3. What is thrown away?Type a few letters in the search box, tap one of the products that are thrown away most often, or choose a category. Cannot find it? Type the name and tap Use as free text.
  4. WeightEnter the weight. Tap g or kg first to choose the unit; the app remembers it.
  5. ReasonTap why it is thrown away, for example overproduction, spoiled, plate waste or preparation waste.
  6. SaveAs soon as everything is filled in, the green Save button appears at the bottom with the weight on it. Tap it. Done.
More details (optional). Under More details you can add the dish, the supplier, the location, the moment (breakfast, lunch, dinner, ...), a note, or a different time if you register something later. Only fill this in when your manager asks for it.

3. Photos and the AI suggestion

When you add a photo, the app looks at it and suggests what it is, the category and sometimes the reason. You see the suggestion in a green box with how sure the app is. Tap Use this to accept it, or simply choose something else.

Good photos

  • One type of product per photo
  • Taken from above, in good light
  • The whole amount in view
  • Plates with leftovers: photograph the plate, the app recognises plate waste

Less useful

  • A full mixed bin
  • Dark or blurry photos
  • Only part of the product visible
  • Photos with people's faces on them
The suggestion is a help, not the truth. Always check it before you save. You decide.

4. Weighing well

  • Weigh the waste, not the container. Put the empty container on the scale and press tare (zero) first, or subtract the weight of the container.
  • One product per registration where you can. Ten kilos of "mixed" tells the kitchen less than three registrations of bread, salad and sauce.
  • Register right away, at the moment you throw it away. Afterwards you forget things.
  • Small amounts count too. A tray of herbs every day adds up over a month.

5. After saving

Your registration appears at the bottom of the page under Your registrations today. The form is empty again, ready for the next one.

If your organisation uses the leaderboard, the app may ask: "How could this have been prevented?" One short sentence is enough, for example "Make the second batch only after 12:00". Good ideas earn points for your section. You can also skip the question.

6. Fixing a mistake

You can correct or delete your own registrations on the same day. Go to Records and tap Edit or Delete next to the registration. After that day, ask your manager.

8. Questions

I do not see my restaurant or section.

Your manager decides which restaurants you can see. Ask them to tick your restaurant in Settings › Users.

The app looks different from yesterday.

The app is updated regularly. If something looks odd, close it and open it again, or refresh the page.

Can my manager see who registered what?

Yes, managers can see registrations per person. The goal is to learn as a team, not to blame anyone. Throwing less away starts with honest numbers.

I have no signal in the kitchen.

The app needs an internet connection to save. Use the kitchen Wi-Fi, or register as soon as you have signal again and set the right time under More details.

Who do I ask for help?

Your kitchen manager or the WASTE log contact person in your organisation.
